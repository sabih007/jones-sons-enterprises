About Jones & Sons

A family business that thinks in decades.

Jones & Sons Enterprises is a family-owned American holding company, founded in 2018 by Terrill James Jones and Deanna Garcia.

From Rancho Palos Verdes, the family owns and manages businesses across commercial real estate, healthcare and dental care, business management, investment holdings, sports and entertainment, and memorabilia.

The idea behind the company is simple. Build businesses with integrity, invest in innovation that actually helps people, and choose partners for the long term. Every brand in the group carries the family name, so every brand is held to the family standard.

That standard reaches past the balance sheet. The companies we run serve Southern California neighborhoods, and we measure our success by the difference they make there.
